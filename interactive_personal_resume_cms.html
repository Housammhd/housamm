Research Profile & Executive Summary

Doctor of Engineering and Research Assistant at Brno University of Technology, specializing in Design Engineering, Non-Destructive Testing (NDT), Condition Monitoring (CM), and Acoustic Emission (AE) diagnostics. Extensive experimental track record analyzing Rolling Contact Fatigue (RCF), bearing defect propagation in toroidal and low-speed roller bearings, and semi-active magnetorheological damping systems. Proven educator instructing university coursework in Machine Design and CAD methodologies.
